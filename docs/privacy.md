# Privacy policy

*Effective 31 October 2026*

Brewery Pi is a process-data historian for breweries: brewery staff use it,
through Claude, to record and look up readings and batches. This page explains
what Brewery Pi keeps about the people who use it, and why.

## What we keep about you

When you sign in, Brewery Pi receives your **name** and **email address** from
the sign-in service you chose -- Google, Microsoft, or a one-time code sent to
your email. Sign-in is handled for us by [WorkOS](https://workos.com). From
Google we receive only your basic profile: your name, email address, and
whether that email is verified. We never receive your password.

Brewery Pi also stores:

- the **identifier** your sign-in service gives your account, so you are
  recognised the next time you sign in;
- your **role** at each brewery you have access to (operator or
  administrator), and which sites you can see;
- your **timezone**, if you choose to set one.

## What you record

The readings, batches and equipment settings you enter belong to your brewery.
Brewery Pi stores them so you and your colleagues can look them up later.

## How we use it

Only to recognise you when you sign in, to decide what you are allowed to see
and change, and to show times in your timezone. We do not sell your
information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the
services that run Brewery Pi:

- **WorkOS** -- sign-in;
- **Hetzner** -- the server Brewery Pi runs on;
- **Anthropic** -- you use Brewery Pi through Claude, so what you ask and what
  Brewery Pi answers passes through Claude, under
  [Anthropic's own privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

Brewery Pi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## How long we keep it

As long as you have access to Brewery Pi. When you leave a brewery, an
administrator deactivates your account so you can no longer sign in; your
records stay with the brewery. To have your account deleted entirely, email
us.

## Contact

Questions, or a request to see or delete what Brewery Pi holds about you:
[brewerypiit@gmail.com](mailto:brewerypiit@gmail.com).
